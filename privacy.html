Form Keeper Privacy Policy

Form Keeper is designed with absolute privacy in mind. The extension does not collect, transmit, distribute, sell, or monitor your data. All form inputs, global profiles, and user settings are stored strictly locally on your device using your browser's native local storage.

The extension contains no analytics tracking, no remote code, and makes zero network requests. Your data never leaves your machine. Passwords and explicitly sensitive fields are never saved. You retain full control to export or permanently delete your data at any time via the extension's settings.
