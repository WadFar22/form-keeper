Form Keeper - Local Form AutoFill

Stop typing the same data over and over.

Form Keeper is a zero-telemetry, local-first browser extension that saves and instantly autofills repetitive forms. Never lose data to a crashed tab again.

Kill Repetitive Work

Save an exact layout for a recurring portal or map your details onto any random checkout page. Fill complex forms in a single click.

Crash-Proof Forms

Form Keeper tracks your typing in the background. If your browser crashes or a tab closes, your data is restored instantly.

100% Local-First

No cloud, no accounts, and strictly no telemetry. Everything lives entirely on your device using your browser's local storage.

Privacy-Hardened

The extension actively ignores password fields and sensitive inputs. The codebase is fully open-source for absolute transparency.

How it works

1

Enable Recording

Open the extension popup and toggle on "Record this site entries" for any website where you want to start tracking forms.

2

Fill & Save

Fill out your web form normally. Click the floating "Save" button or use the extension popup to capture the layout.

3

Right-Click to Fill

The next time you visit, simply right-click anywhere on the page to instantly inject your saved data, or use the popup.

4

Stay Offline

Enjoy a faster workflow knowing none of your keystrokes or form data are ever transmitted to a remote server.
