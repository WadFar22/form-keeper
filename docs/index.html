How it works

1

Enable Recording

Open the extension popup and toggle on "Record this site entries" for any website where you want to start tracking forms.

2

Fill & Save

Fill out your web form normally. Click the floating "Save" button or use the extension popup to capture the exact layout.

3

Right-Click to Fill

The next time you visit, simply right-click anywhere on the page to instantly inject your saved data, or use the popup.

4

Stay Offline

Enjoy a radically faster workflow knowing none of your keystrokes or form data are ever transmitted to a remote server.
