Form Keeper — User Guide

A simple guide to saving time and never typing out the same forms twice.

Getting Started: How to Use Form Keeper

Form Keeper works seamlessly in the background to ensure you never lose your typed data.

  • 1
    Turn on Recording: Open the Form Keeper popup on any website and toggle the "Record entries for this site" switch to the ON position.
  • 2
    Silent Backups: As you type into any text box, the extension silently records your progress in the background as a temporary draft.
  • 3
    Save Your Work: Click the floating save button on the page to save instantly. Alternatively, open the extension and click + Save current form as new entry to save it for that specific site, or switch to the Profiles tab and click + Save current form as a Profile to save it for universal use across the web.
  • 4
    Crash Recovery: If the browser crashes or you accidentally close a tab, simply return to the page, open the extension, and click Restore on the green ↻ Unsaved Draft (Recovery) entry to instantly get your typing back.

Understanding the Two Ways to Save Data

Form Keeper organizes your saved inputs into two distinct tools to fit different situations:

1. Global Profiles (Universal)

Saved across all websites. Ideal for your universal personal details (Full Name, Phone, Email, Address, Date of Birth). Form Keeper automatically maps these fields onto almost any form you encounter anywhere on the web.

2. Saved Forms (Site-Specific)

Saved strictly for one specific website. Perfect for recurring forms, tax portals, or complex dashboards. It remembers the exact structure of that webpage (even distinguishing language versions like /en or /ar) for a 100% exact refill.

Right-Click Shortcuts

You can fill entire forms or inject single pieces of data directly without opening the extension popup. Simply right-click on the webpage to access the unified menu:

  • ✦
    Fill an Entire Form: Right-click and choose either Fill from Saved Profile or Fill from Saved Forms. Hover over your desired saved entry and click ▶ Fill this Profile (or ▶ Fill this entire form) to populate all matching boxes on the page at once.
  • ✦
    Fill a Single Field: Right-click directly inside a specific input box on the page. Under your Profiles or Saved Forms, bypass the main fill button and simply click the exact piece of data you want (e.g., First Name: John). Form Keeper will inject only that specific value into the active text box.

Data Control: Exporting & Importing

You have absolute control over your saved information. Access these options by clicking Settings (Manage) in the extension popup and navigating to the General Settings & Data Control tab.

Export Options

Back up your data to your local hard drive. You can export a full JSON backup (perfect for restoring data later or moving to a new computer) or a human-readable CSV spreadsheet to review your saved fields manually.

Import & Restore

Click Import from file to upload a previously saved JSON backup or a custom-formatted CSV file. Form Keeper will automatically merge the imported data with your existing entries without overwriting your current progress.

Privacy & Security

  • ✓
    100% On-Device: All of your entries and profiles live purely inside your browser's local storage. Nothing is ever sent to a remote server or cloud.
  • ✓
    Passwords are Never Saved: Form Keeper strictly ignores password fields under all circumstances.
  • ✓
    Full Deletion: You can wipe a single site's history or completely erase all stored data instantly from the Settings menu.
